# Luminostalgic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Luminostalgic is a browser extension made by Echo5VGames (Arzu Rustamova). It shows YouTube's picture-in-picture video inside retro device themes. This policy explains what the extension does and does not do with your information.

## What stays on your computer

- **Your settings** (on/off, chosen theme, layout, Fill screen, Interactive mode, and whether the extension has run on YouTube yet) are saved in your browser's extension storage. They are never sent anywhere.
- **The video you are watching.** To show the video, title, and progress on the device, the extension reads the current YouTube video and its title from the page. This happens only inside your browser. Nothing about what you watch is collected, stored, or sent.

## Payments

Luminostalgic is free. It takes no payments and uses no license keys or payment services, and the extension does not contact any server.

## What we do not do

- No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
- No selling or sharing of personal data.
- No collection of your browsing history or YouTube activity.
