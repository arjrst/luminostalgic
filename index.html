Click YouTube's picture-in-picture button as usual. Your video floats in the device you picked and stays on top while you work.

Questions

Which browsers work?

Chrome 116 or newer on desktop.

How do I unlock Pro?

After purchase, Lemon Squeezy emails you a license key. Paste it into the extension popup.

I installed it but nothing happens.

If YouTube was already open when you installed, refresh the page once. Then click YouTube's picture-in-picture button as usual.

Can I use my key on a new computer?

One key works on up to 3 devices. To move it, click "Move to another computer" in the popup, then activate it on the new one. If you can't, email me and I'll free a slot.

What data does it collect?

None. No tracking, no analytics, no ads. The only thing sent out is your license key, to check it with Lemon Squeezy. Read the privacy policy.
